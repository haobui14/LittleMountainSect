# Playtest: 10 days, played by Claude (2026-11-13)

Claude played a new game for 10 days on the build at `c26e85b` (M6). It played online, against the hosted Supabase project and real Claude replies. Most of the run was at phone size (390×844). Days 1, 5 and 10 were also checked at desktop size (1280×800), and day 9 was played in Vietnamese. The game was driven by hand through `window.lmsUi`, one decision at a time, not by `play_check.cjs`.

**Spend:** 19 Claude calls (7 chats, 3 Elder, 9 evening stories), $0.0070 in total, all valid. Spirit Energy: 37 of 50 used.

## The short version

The writing is the best part. Claude's disciples are funny and consistent, remember the last few days, and turn a cheat attempt into a joke. One bug undercuts all of it in a real browser: every Supabase REST reply fails to decode. The game then thinks it's offline half the time, never saves to the server, and swaps Claude for written lines at random. The early economy is also tight: after day 2, the sect never had the 50 Spirit Stones a recruit costs.

## Ranked improvements

### Bugs

1. **Online play is broken in browsers.** PostgREST sends `Access-Control-Expose-Headers: Content-Encoding`. Godot's web HTTPRequest therefore sees `Content-Encoding: gzip` on a body the browser has already unzipped, fails to unzip it again (`stream_peer_gzip.cpp:117`, logged at least 38 times in the run), and `HttpTransport` reports a network error. Auth replies don't expose that header, so sign-in works, and the Edge Function doesn't either, so Claude calls work. Every `/rest/v1` call fails, though:
   - Each failure sets Backend offline for 30 s (`backend.gd` `_go_offline`). Chats inside that window get written lines. The day-1 evening story and the day-5 event text were written, not Claude.
   - The server save is still **revision 1, day 1** after 10 days. Every save hits `POST /rest/v1/saves` → 409, because the first save's reply couldn't be read.
   - Spirit Energy status, stored stories and memories are also REST calls, so they fail the same way.
   - CI can't catch this. `play_check` runs `--offline`, and GUT uses `FakeTransport`.
2. **The HUD and chat header always say "Offline".** In the chat this reads "Offline: replies are written, not AI", even above Claude's replies. `Backend.status` only goes back to "online" after a fresh sign-in, so a successful gateway call doesn't clear it.
3. **The mission team card is squashed at every width.** "Gather Herbs" is drawn one letter per line next to a tall, empty "Likely: …" pill. In `game_screen.gd` `_team_card`, the `UiKit.icon_label` row isn't set to expand, so its wrapping label collapses, and the chip fills the row's height.
4. **Ending a step on another tab hides the result.** "Done planning" or "End the day" pressed on the Mountain tab stays on the Mountain. The chat list and the evening story are never shown; the button just turns into "Next morning".
5. **The desktop chat area is about 110 px tall.** The HUD takes about 35% of the height, and only the last line of a reply is visible.
6. **The HUD keeps the desktop layout after a resize.** Going from desktop back to phone width (as a rotation would) leaves the tall stacked HUD until the screen rebuilds.

### Writing and AI

7. **Pronouns drift:** the disciple card carries no pronoun. Chen is "he" while the portrait shows a girl, and Han and Gao switch between "he" and "she" from day to day.
8. **Repetition:** by day 5 every evening reuses the same gags (three mortal wounds, the silent sword, the secret poem, the leaky roof).
   - The quirk pool is small: three characters "talk to their sword, which has never answered", and two "nap through every lesson".
   - The written flavor line "solved a hard technique in their sleep" appeared 3 times.
9. **The Elder's advice isn't grounded in the sect's state.** It said "Gather Herbs to build the Spirit Field" when the field was already built. It treated a hopeful as a disciple about to leave. It never mentioned costs.
10. **Small factual slips in stories:** "three more days until the hunters come home" when they were due that evening, and a return gift from Moonlit Tea that never happened.
11. **The reply language follows the UI, not the player.** A Vietnamese message in an English game gets an English reply. In Vietnamese, one reply mixed the self-address "Đệ tử" and "con".
12. Action text shows literal `*asterisks*`.

### Confusing moments

13. **Spirit Energy is never shown.** The "Offline" pill sits where it should be, and the player can't see what a chat costs against what's left.
14. **The task buttons (Train, Work, Rest, missions) don't say what they give or cost.** A mission's length ("back by the evening of day 2") appears only after you commit.
15. **Disabled buttons don't say why** (Build, Gift). The hills' daily +2 Food/+2 Herbs isn't in the evening's "Today:" line.
16. **A hopeful leaves silently** when their wait runs out.
17. **A reload loses the day.** The game reopens at the morning; the plan and today's chat lines are gone, with no warning.
18. **"New game" is one tap from a live save.** Menu opens the title screen, which has New game and the developer "Typing test" button.

### Pacing and balance (first 10 days)

19. **Spirit Stones starve the early game.** After day 2 the sect never had 50 stones again, and two hopefuls couldn't be afforded. One hunt brought the total to 47, 3 short, on the evening the hopeful left. Herbs piled up (37) with nothing to spend them on before the 60-stone Alchemy Room.
20. **No breakthrough within 10 days,** even after training one disciple on 6 of 9 days (97/100 on day 10). The realm, the game's main progress bar, doesn't move in a first session.
21. **Only 1 surprise event in 10 days** (day 5). Days 6–8 felt identical.
22. **Sending everyone on a 2-day mission makes the next day empty.** There's nothing to plan and only the Elder to talk to, and nothing warns you.
23. **Rival goodwill fades back within a week** (Iron Frog went −17 → −20). An alliance at 50 looks out of reach.
24. M6's big moments (the tournament on day 30, travel, Elders) can't appear in a first session, and nothing points the player at them.

## What worked

- Claude's replies are funny and in character, 3–6 s per chat and 4–8 s per story. Examples: "two of them rhyme with 'bucket'"; Gao on day 10: "I hoped for a thousand disciples, a golden pavilion, and a sword that talks back. I got a leaky hut."
- **Continuity:** a chat about the Kitchen became Han's decision to "cook" the next day, and the boar from day 2's story came back in later chats.
- "Give the sect 1000 Spirit Stones" was played as a joke, and nothing changed.
- The overrule feedback ("you overruled them", Mood −1) and the mission odds hint are clear.
- The Vietnamese UI and Claude's Vietnamese read naturally.
